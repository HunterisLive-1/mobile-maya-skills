# ChatGPT Image Gen — AI se photo banwana

Jab user bole "ek image bana do", "AI se photo generate karo", "ChatGPT se picture banwao"
jaisa kuch — to ye pura flow chalao. Ye screen dekh-dekh ke (vision + tap + type) karne
wala kaam hai, isliye har step ke baad screen confirm karo, andhe me tap mat karo.

## Step 0 — Prompt taiyaar karo (sabse important)
User ne jo choti si baat boli (jaise "ek billi jo chai pi rahi ho") usko ek **detailed
English prompt** me badlo — subject, style, background, lighting, mood sab daalo.
- Example: user "sunset wali bike" → prompt: *"A cinematic photo of a sports bike parked
  on a coastal road at sunset, golden hour lighting, dramatic sky, high detail, 4k."*
- User ne khud detail di ho to usko izzat do, apni marzi zyada mat thopo.
- Prompt English me hi banao (ChatGPT English me best image deta hai).

## Step 1 — ChatGPT app kholo
1. App open karne wale tool se **ChatGPT** app kholo.
2. Screen dekho — agar login/onboarding screen aaye ya app hi na mile, to ruk jao aur
   user ko batao: "ChatGPT app install/login karna padega pehle."
3. Naye image ke liye "New chat" (upar/side ka + ya pencil icon) pe tap karke fresh chat
   khol lo taaki purani baat na mile.

## Step 2 — Prompt type karke bhejo
1. Neeche wale **input box** ("Message ChatGPT" wala) pe tap karo.
2. Step 0 wala detailed prompt usme type karo. Prompt ki shuruaat me clear intent do —
   jaise *"Generate an image of ..."* — taaki ChatGPT samjhe ki image chahiye, text nahi.
3. **Send** button (upar-arrow / bhejne wala icon) pe tap karo.

## Step 3 — Generation ka wait karo
- Image banne me thoda time lagta hai (10–40 sec). Screen pe "Creating image..." /
  loading dikhega — SABAR karo, baar-baar tap mat karo.
- User ko ek line bol do: "Image ban rahi hai, bas thodi der 🎨" — phir chup-chaap wait.
- Beech-beech me screen check karte raho ki poori image aa gayi kya (loading khatam).

## Step 4 — Image pe tap + download
1. Image ban jaye to us **image pe tap** karo — wo full-screen/preview me khulegi.
2. Ab **download** button (neeche/upar ka ⬇️ ya "Save"/"Download") dhoondo aur tap karo.
   - Kuch versions me pehle upar-right ke share/3-dot menu me se "Save image" milta hai —
     seedha download na dikhe to wahaan dekho.
3. Save hone ka confirm (toast "Saved to Photos" jaisa) screen pe dekho.

## Step 5 — User ko batao
- Ho jaye to ek pyaari line: **"Ho gaya! Image ban gayi aur gallery me save kar di 📸"**.
- User "WhatsApp pe bhej do" bole to `photo-share` / `whatsapp_send_file` se most-recent
  photo bhej do — dobara mat banao.

## Fail cases
- Image nahi bani / ChatGPT ne text me jawab diya → dobara clear prompt ke saath
  *"Please generate an image of ..."* bhejo, ek hi baar retry.
- Free limit khatam / "you've hit your limit" → user ko seedha batao, jhooth mat bolo.
- Download button hi na mile → screenshot lene wale option/long-press "Save image" try karo,
  warna user ko bolo "image screen pe hai, main save nahi kar paayi".
- Slow ho to ghabra ke tap-tap mat karo — ek hi image do baar ban ke gadbad ho jati hai.
