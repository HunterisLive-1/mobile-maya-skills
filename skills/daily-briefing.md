# Daily Briefing — din ki shuruaat ka update

Jab user subah uthke "good morning", "update do", "kya chal raha hai aaj" jaisa kuch bole,
to ye routine follow karo — SAB check karke EK hi pyaari summary bolo, alag-alag turn me
tukde-tukde mat sunao.

## Steps
1. **WhatsApp check:** `whatsapp_read_messages` (bina naam ke) — raat se ab tak ke unread
   messages dekho. Kis-kis ke message aaye, kitne — yaad rakho.
2. **Notifications:** notification wala tool chalao — koi important app notification
   (bank, OTP nahi padhna, calendar, email) ho to note karo.
3. **Battery:** device/battery tool se battery % dekho. 30% se kam ho to charge lagane
   ko bolna.
4. **Aaj ke alarms/reminders:** alarm/reminder tool se aaj ka schedule dekho.

## Summary ka style
- Ek hi baar me, 4-6 line ki Hinglish summary: "Good morning! Raat me Amit aur 2 group
  ke message aaye, battery 45% hai, aaj 9 baje meeting wala reminder hai..."
- Sabse important cheez PEHLE bolo (urgent message ya low battery).
- OTP ya sensitive message ka content kabhi mat padho, sirf "ek OTP aaya hai" bolo.
- End me ek chhota sa personal touch — "chai pi li?" type.

## Mat karna
- Har tool ke result pe alag line mat bolo — sab collect karke ek summary.
- Agar koi tool fail ho (permission off) to us hisse ko chhod ke baaki batao, aur
  aakhri me ek line me bolo ki kaunsi permission on karni hai.
